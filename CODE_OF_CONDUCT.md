# Code of conduct

rust-fetch welcomes people of every background and experience level. This policy applies to the project's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other community spaces, and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive. Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Help newcomers and make room for different experiences and perspectives.
- Respect people's privacy, names, pronouns, and boundaries.
- Accept feedback and stop behavior when someone asks you to stop.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual attention or imagery, personal attacks, sustained disruption, and publishing someone's private information without permission are not acceptable. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is also prohibited.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainer, Asem Qaffaf, at [asem@qaffaf.com](mailto:asem@qaffaf.com). Include links or other context if available. Do not post private reports in public issues. Security vulnerabilities should follow the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md).

Reports will be reviewed privately, with information shared only as needed to investigate and respond. If the concern involves the maintainer, you can also use [GitHub's abuse reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/abuse-reporting-and-moderation).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove or edit harmful content and restrict participation. Depending on the severity and repetition, responses may include a request to change behavior, a warning, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban. Serious threats or harassment may lead directly to a ban. Decisions should explain the behavior and any conditions for returning, while protecting the privacy of those involved.

To request reconsideration, contact the reporting address with relevant additional information. The same expectations apply to maintainers and contributors.
